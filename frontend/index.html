Instagram Mutuals

Some follow you. Some don't. Find out who.

Instagram doesn't let you see, in one place, which accounts you follow don't follow you back, or which of your followers you haven't followed back. This tool reads your own exported Instagram data and sorts everyone into three groups: mutual follows, people you follow who don't follow you, and people who follow you who you don't follow.

  1. Open Instagram → Settings and activity
  2. Accounts Centre
  3. Your information and permissions
  4. Export your information
  5. Create export
  6. Choose Export to device
  7. Under date range, select All time
  8. Tap Customise information
  9. Under Connections, uncheck everything except Followers and Following
  10. Submit, wait for Instagram's email, then download the zip Instagram sends you
  11. Upload that zip directly below — no need to extract it yourself
Safe to use. Your file is analyzed in your browser session and never saved anywhere — not on a server, not in a database. Nothing about your account is stored after you close this page.
Not real-time. Results only reflect the export you upload. Instagram doesn't let any app check your live followers/following list. Re-export and re-upload whenever you want fresh numbers.
